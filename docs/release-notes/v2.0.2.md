A new way to find and add widgets.

- **Add widget, redesigned.** In edit layout, Add widget opens a centred sheet over the desktop. Search and your packs are on the left. Every widget appears in each of its sizes as a live preview: drag a size onto the desktop, or click **+** to place it in the first free spot. You can also find a widget with Tab, the arrow keys and Enter.
- **Your widget library.** The control panel's Add widgets page groups widgets by pack, in sections you can collapse. Star favourites to keep them at the top, move packs up or down, hide the ones you don't use, and switch between grid and list view. Vitrine remembers your choices.
- **Cleaner previews.** Every preview sits on the same neutral tile, in the app and in the store.
