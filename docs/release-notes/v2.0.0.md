Vitrine 2 is a complete rewrite.

- **Beneath the icons.** Widgets are drawn under the desktop icons, which stay visible and clickable. This works on Windows 11 24H2/25H2 and on earlier versions.
- **A grid of widgets.** Instead of one full-screen theme, the desktop is a grid of independent widgets. Add, move and resize them from the tray menu or with **Ctrl+Alt+V**.
- **Edit layout.** Your windows step aside while you arrange the desktop. Drag widgets, resize them with live snapping to the grid, and drop new ones from the gallery, with search and package tabs. Every step is animated.
- **Frosted glass.** Widgets can blur the real wallpaper behind them, like Windows 11 acrylic, or float directly on it with soft text shadows. Light and dark follow Windows, live.
- **Control panel.** Search and filter widgets by package, size and more, see live previews and pick a size before placing one. Settings changes show up as you make them.
- **Store.** Browse, install and update widget packs from the [Vitrine Store](https://vitrine-org.github.io/packs/) inside the control panel. Every download is checked against its SHA-256.
- **Windows 11 menus.** The tray and widget menus are Fluent flyouts, with acrylic, icons, switches and keyboard navigation.
- **Interactive.** Widgets can react to clicks, hover and the mouse wheel where no icon is in the way. They can also ask for text, for example to add a to-do.
- **In your language.** English, Italiano, Français, Deutsch, Español, Português (Brasil), 简体中文, Русский, 日本語 and 한국어.
- **Light.** A native engine (Rust, Direct2D, DirectComposition, QuickJS) replaces WebView2. There is no browser process, and widgets render only when something changes. Rendering pauses while the screen is locked or a full-screen app is running.
- **Packages.** Widgets come in `.vitrine` packages; double-click one to install it. **Essentials** is included, with 13 widgets:
  - clock and analog clock;
  - calendar, to-do, note, links, countdown and progress;
  - system, drives, network, battery and weather.

  The Vitrine 1 themes return as packs in the store: Sargas, Antares, Mond and Alnitak.
- **Build your own** with JSX and SolidJS: see [the SDK](https://github.com/vitrine-org/sdk) and the [documentation](https://vitrine-org.github.io/docs/introduction/).

Vitrine 1 themes are not compatible with Vitrine 2. Installing Vitrine 2 leaves your Vitrine 1 settings and themes untouched. Vitrine 1 remains available on the `v1` branch.
