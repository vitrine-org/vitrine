Vitrine 2 is a complete rewrite.

- **Beneath the icons.** Widgets are drawn under the desktop icons, which stay visible and clickable on Windows 11 24H2/25H2 and on earlier versions.
- **A grid of widgets.** Instead of one full-screen theme, the desktop is a grid: add, move and resize independent widgets from the tray menu or with **Ctrl+Alt+V**.
- **Edit layout.** Your windows step aside while you arrange the desktop: drag, resize with live snapping to the grid, and drop new widgets from the gallery, all animated.
- **Control panel.** Browse widgets with live previews, pick a size before placing one, and see settings changes as you make them.
- **Transparent or carded.** Widgets can sit on an acrylic card or float directly on the wallpaper, with soft text shadows for legibility. Light and dark follow Windows.
- **Interactive.** Widgets can react to clicks, hover and the mouse wheel where no icon is in the way.
- **Light.** A native engine (Rust, Direct2D, DirectComposition, QuickJS) replaces WebView2: no browser process, rendering only when something changes, paused while the screen is locked or a full-screen app is running.
- **Packages.** Widgets come in `.vitrine` packages; double-click one to install it. The **Essentials** pack (clock, calendar, system, network, battery, weather) is included; the Vitrine 1 themes return as packs: [Sargas](https://github.com/vitrine-org/sargas/releases), [Antares](https://github.com/vitrine-org/antares/releases), [Mond](https://github.com/vitrine-org/mond/releases) and [Alnitak](https://github.com/vitrine-org/alnitak/releases).
- **Build your own** with JSX and SolidJS: see [the SDK](https://github.com/vitrine-org/sdk) and the [documentation](https://vitrine-org.github.io/).

Vitrine 1 themes are not compatible with Vitrine 2; your Vitrine 1 settings and themes are left untouched. Vitrine 1 remains available on the `v1` branch.
